MEGA65 PLAY RECORDER

Browser-based recording and BASIC 65 PLAY export.

VERSION 0.5 · EXPERIMENTAL

What is it?

MEGA65 PLAY Recorder is a standalone browser tool for recording melodies with a computer keyboard or the on-screen piano and converting them into BASIC 65 PLAY data.

The recording can be replayed, edited and quantized in the browser before being exported as a PLAY string or as a ready-to-use BASIC program.

Main features

  • Record notes in the browser.
  • Use the computer keyboard or the on-screen piano.
  • Shift the visible keyboard range by octaves.
  • Edit pitch, start time and note duration.
  • Quantize recorded timing.
  • Preview the edited result.
  • Generate BASIC 65 PLAY strings and programs.
  • Choose monophonic or experimental two-voice export.

Two-voice export

In two-voice mode, ordinary single notes are placed in Voice 1. When two notes overlap, the higher note remains in Voice 1 and the lower note is placed in Voice 2.

If more than two notes overlap, additional middle notes are omitted.

Experimental software

This program is experimental and not perfect.

Longer recordings may cause the editor and export functions to become noticeably slower. Recordings will also often require manual timing adjustments afterwards.

Some timing corrections can be made directly in the browser by editing note start times and durations before exporting.

Recommended workflow

  • Record at a comfortable and steady tempo.
  • Use quantization to clean up the performance.
  • Review the recorded note list.
  • Adjust note timing and duration where needed.
  • Preview the edited sequence.
  • Export and test the result on the MEGA65.

Important limitation

MEGA65 PLAY Recorder is not a full MIDI sequencer. It attempts to translate a live performance into readable and usable BASIC 65 PLAY data, but manual fine-tuning is probably required. It is conceived by a person without any musical knowledge.

Privacy and compatibility

READY. THE TOOL RUNS LOCALLY IN YOUR BROWSER. NO RECORDING DATA IS UPLOADED OR SENT ANYWHERE. A MODERN DESKTOP BROWSER IS RECOMMENDED.
